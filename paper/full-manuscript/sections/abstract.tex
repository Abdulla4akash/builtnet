\begin{abstract}
A population register records memberships and relations from which analysts construct a network. Different construction rules can change the quantities used to compare networks. We describe \builtnet{}, a Python package with \CardCount{} executable construction cards covering \BaseCardCount{} reference rule sets, and illustrate their application to one membership input.
All \TestCount{} release tests passed. On the \SmallPeople{}-person example the Dutch 2018, Danish hard-cap and Swedish cards gave mean union degrees of \SmallDutchDegree{}, \SmallDanishDegree{} and \SmallSwedishDegree{} over their respective eligible people.
The registered Danish hard cap gave a signed relative error of \DanishCappedError\% against the published colleague edge total. The post hoc two-way nomination improved the fit to \DanishTwoWayError\% but conflicted with the source's description of individual degree. Two Dutch secondary-school targets also failed. Different excess-closure definitions gave \ClosurePaper{}, \ClosureLibrary{} and \ClosureTiePair{} for the same two-layer triangle.
The package supports controlled comparisons of stated construction rules across \LayerCount{} implemented layer types. The examples reach \MainPeople{} people and assess reproducible projection. Individual contact validity and nationwide throughput remain outside their scope.
\end{abstract}
