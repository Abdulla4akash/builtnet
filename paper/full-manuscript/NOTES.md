# Author notes

## One-sentence claim

builtnet makes construction choices in register-based population networks inspectable and testable by encoding source-attributed alternatives as executable cards that can be applied to the same membership records.

## Title options

1. builtnet: Executable construction rules for register-based population networks
2. From register memberships to network ties: Construction cards in builtnet
3. Auditing population network construction with executable rule catalogues

## Outline

- Methods: membership contract, construction and provenance, layer operators and descriptors, validation design and timing boundaries.
- Results: deterministic regression checks, same-input card example, exact and approximate predictions, ambiguity cases, practical runtime.
- Related work: register releases and their applications, projection and multilayer analysis, synthetic contacts and comparison, research software and documentation.
- Discussion: what executable comparisons establish, source ambiguity and restricted-data validity, failure modes and limitations.
- Supplement: implementation catalogue, resource measurements, provenance, failures and practical reproduction.

## Disagreements and decisions

- The README quick start says about 9,600 people and computes a plain clustering mean over active rows. Seed 1 yielded 9,729 people. The ordinary mean is undefined for three small-scale cards. The manuscript retains those outputs and reports the conditional mean with its defined-case denominator. The first timing wrapper stopped when comparing two undefined means for equality. The corrected capture encodes them as null and uses the same example, seeds, operators and work sizes.
- Earlier package logs show an import failure due to missing pandas, followed by one skipped case. The manuscript uses the later local run with the optional dependencies present, which exercised all 79 collected cases. Historical igraph parity is separate from the pytest count.
- `builtnet-tool` and the companion `STATUS.md` summarise prediction performance tersely. The later `THEORY.md` fresh-seed section controls the nearest-home verdict. The clustering error was −0.061 against the 0.06 criterion and therefore failed narrowly. The 1% statement in the short draft applies to the group checks and has no bearing on the broader approximations.
- The Danish supplement places fully linked small sites at 100 employees or fewer and describes fewer than 100 realised colleagues for some workers in sampled sites. The cards share a default clique threshold of cap plus one. The later companion JSON verifies 86.844929 million for the hard-cap calculation and 122.013682 million for the two-way reading against 125.07 million published. The relative errors are −30.6% and −2.4%. The denominator is the published total, resolving ambiguous wording in `docs/rule-sets.md` and the Danish addendum narrative.
- The Dutch yearly colleague catalogue and the CBS 2024 application give different cap scopes across employers. The card omits monthly pooling and implements a single employer per person.
- Dutch applications describe a 100-classmate nomination option. The official 2018 discussion paper gives outgoing school quantiles beyond 100, so the baseline remains uncapped and the other reading remains an explicitly named sensitivity. The companion adult-cohort discussion and Swedish construction scripts differ over the cohort window, an assumption in the base Swedish card.
- The original card catalogue labels some sources as current 2026 documents. The bibliography uses the opened June 2024 CBS codebook editions with the relevant rule descriptions. The manuscript accounts for the base Swedish omissions visible in the operator as well as those listed in TOML.
- The short draft used a different affiliation. This draft uses the applicant's requested author block. The named user-space TeX path worked, although its executables report TeX Live 2026. The module `apps/gcc/texlive/2025-03-07` exposes `latexmk` but lacks a module-local `pdflatex`, so the checked build used the requested user-space path.

## Build and reproduction

The paper rebuilds from the checked aggregate JSON and the package's theory JSON. From this folder use Python 3.11 or later for \`src/check_numbers.py --check\`, \`src/make_tables.py\` and \`src/make_figures.py\`. The recorded plotting environment used Matplotlib 3.11.2. Build with \`pdflatex\`, \`bibtex\`, then \`pdflatex\` until the label warning clears. The checked PDF has 34 pages, 59 citations in the bibliography, 16 figure labels and 20 table labels. All figure and table exports regenerated byte for byte.

\`src/measure_examples.py\` is separate from the build. Run it from the repository root with \`PYTHONPATH=src\` on a two-core allocation to repeat the existing examples. The test environment included pytest 9.2.0, pandas 3.1.1, mlnlib 0.3.0 and scikit-learn 1.9.1 alongside the recorded NumPy and SciPy versions. Repeating the capture will produce new wall times.

The numerical checker also verified the manuscript's companion subset directly against the archived \`predictions.json\` and \`posthoc.json\`. Neither source file is copied into the paper folder.
